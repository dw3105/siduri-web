# Human in the loop

Machines do the work. A person stays at the gate.

I build small software systems with agents and keep a human responsible for what becomes public. Siduri is the record: tools built, failures understood, and useful software shipped with the boundary visible.

The site is the artifact. Its source is Markdown, its output is static HTML, and its checks are part of the thing I ship. I write down the tools, the durations, the costs, and the dead ends so the method can be inspected rather than trusted on presentation.

The home page also carries the recent journal, so a stranger can see what has actually happened before deciding whether the method is worth following.
