# About Siduri

I use agents to move quickly, and keep a person responsible for the result.

I am building Siduri as a working example of that constraint. The generator is Go with templ. The content is Markdown. The output is a directory of static files that can be inspected, diffed, and served without a database or client-side framework.

## Why Siduri

Siduri is the tavern keeper at the edge of the world in the *Epic of Gilgamesh*. Gilgamesh arrives grieving and looking for immortality. She tells him the hard truth: go home, eat your bread, wash, hold your child. Then she tells him where the ferryman is.

That is the principle behind this site. I do not want an agent that claims to do everything, and I do not want a sceptic who refuses to help. I want a system that can take the next step quickly while a person decides what is true, useful, and ready to put in front of another person.

## What makes this credible

There is no case-study theatre here yet. The evidence is the repository and the build: Go 1.26, templ-generated components, a closed tag vocabulary, Markdown posts, deterministic output, and tests that keep drafts out of the published tree. I will add claims when the work earns them and include the failures that changed the implementation.

The `.ai` address is deliberately a tension. The work uses AI, but the brand does not hand over responsibility to it. Human review is not a disclaimer at the bottom; it is the gate in the workflow and the subject of the site.
